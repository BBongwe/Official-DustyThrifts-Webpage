Your checkout update

This page does not confirm payment or reserve your items. We confirm orders only after verifying payment and checking availability.

If you completed payment

Check your inbox for an order confirmation or an order-review email. If a one-of-a-kind item has already sold, we will contact you to arrange a full refund for the affected basket.

Already paid? Please do not pay again while waiting. Check your spam folder, then contact us if the email has not arrived or anything looks incorrect.

What happens next

Confirmed, allocated orders are dispatched within 2 working days after payment, excluding weekends and South African public holidays. Courier transit time is additional.

After dispatch, PEP Paxi sends collection updates by SMS. Aramex sends shipment updates by SMS or email. These updates come from your courier using the recipient details supplied with your order.

If you did not complete payment

You can return to the shop. If you are unsure whether you were charged, check with Yoco or contact us before trying again.
